# Origins Cocoa — B2B Positioning, Site Structure & SEO Strategy

Research-backed plan for a fine-flavour Indian cacao brand selling **beans (and derivatives) in bulk to chocolate makers, chocolatiers, and food manufacturers worldwide**. Compiled from live competitor and buyer-intent research (via agent-reach). Goal: present the brand so cleanly and credibly that a buyer landing from Google immediately trusts it and requests a sample/quote.

_Last researched: 2026-08-02._

---

## 1. Who the buyers are (and what they believe)

B2B cacao buyers fall into four personas. The site must serve all four without clutter.

| Persona | What they buy | What they care about most |
|---|---|---|
| **Craft / bean-to-bar makers** (micro → mid) | 1–20 bags → containers | Flavour, fermentation quality, **samples before buying**, story/origin |
| **Chocolatiers & pastry pros** | Beans, nibs, butter, powder | Consistency, tasting notes, small-lot availability |
| **Food manufacturers / private label** | Volume, repeatable lots | Certifications, food safety (COA), consistency, compliance, lead times |
| **Importers / distributors** | Bulk, resale | Price/kg, MOQ, Incoterms, reliable supply |

**Core belief of a serious buyer:** _"Cheap on unit price means nothing. I need the right flavour + verifiable specs + a supplier I can trust to deliver the same lot again."_ They sample first, calculate **landed cost** (not FOB), and demand documentation.

### What they must SEE to trust us (trust signals, in priority order)
1. **Verifiable transparency**, not just the word "traceable" — published data for each step (farm → farmer → harvest → fermentation → drying → grading → packing → shipment). _(This is exactly how the category leader Uncommon Cacao wins.)_
2. **Specs per lot / product** — origin, variety (Trinitario/Criollo/etc.), **fermentation %**, **moisture ≤7.5%**, cut-test / defect rate, bean count per 100g, harvest date, tasting notes.
3. **Certifications & food safety** — Organic (USDA/EU), Fairtrade, Rainforest Alliance, GFSI/food-safety, and **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** incl. heavy metals on request.
4. **Sample availability** + a frictionless **Request a Quote / RFQ**.
5. **Commercial terms up front** — MOQ, price model, **Incoterms (FOB/DDP/EXW)**, lead times, **packaging (jute / GrainPro, 60–70 kg)**, destination countries served.
6. **Consistency & availability** guarantee ("reliable quality, available when you need it").
7. **Farmer + region story with hard numbers** (equity, premiums paid, # farmers).
8. **Proof of quality** — awards, and finished chocolate made from the beans.

## 2. What buyers actually search on Google (keyword map)

Grouped by funnel stage. Target these across pages/content.

**Transactional / commercial (highest value — product & wholesale pages):**
- `buy cacao beans wholesale`, `bulk cocoa beans supplier`, `wholesale cocoa beans [country]`
- `fine flavour cocoa supplier`, `fine flavor cacao beans`
- `single origin cocoa beans wholesale`, `single origin cacao beans supplier`
- `Indian cocoa beans supplier` / `exporter`, `West Godavari cocoa`, `Andhra Pradesh cocoa beans`
- `cocoa beans price per kg`, `cocoa beans MOQ`, `cocoa beans for sale bulk`
- `cocoa nibs wholesale`, `cocoa butter wholesale`, `cocoa powder wholesale` (cross-sell)

**Product / spec (long-tail, per-product pages):**
- `well-fermented cocoa beans`, `fermented and dried cocoa beans`, `cocoa beans moisture 7.5%`
- `Trinitario cacao beans`, `Criollo cacao beans`, `organic cocoa beans wholesale`
- `cocoa beans for bean to bar`, `cacao beans for chocolate making`

**Informational / research (top-funnel — capture with a Journal/Resources hub):**
- `where to buy cacao beans for chocolate making`
- `how to source cocoa beans` / `cocoa sourcing guide`
- `fine flavour vs bulk cacao`, `what is fine flavour cocoa`
- `cocoa fermentation explained`, `cocoa cut test`, `how to evaluate cocoa beans`

**Trust / logistics (support pages + FAQ):**
- `cocoa beans certificate of analysis`, `cocoa Incoterms FOB DDP`, `cocoa phytosanitary certificate`, `cocoa beans jute GrainPro packaging`, `import cocoa beans FDA`

> Note: informational guides (e.g. Santiago Cocoa's "How to buy wholesale cocoa beans", jayarrchocolate, cacaoadventures) currently rank and **funnel buyers to a quote request**. This is an open SEO lane — we should own the Indian/West-Godavari angle of it.

| **Cocoa Supply** (cocoasupply.com) | Wholesale fine-aroma beans | **One rich page per origin/bean** (e.g. Nacional Arriba) — great for SEO |
| **Nutrada** | B2B marketplace | **Filter by origin + fermentation grade**, shows certs (Fairtrade/Rainforest/EU Organic/GFSI), "request bulk quote" |
| **Nahua Cacao** | Single-origin Trinitario | "Award-winning", "meticulous post-harvest" framing |

| **Directories** (wholesalecocoabeans.com, GlobalLinker, go4WorldBusiness, ExportersIndia) | Marketplaces | **Get listed** for referral traffic + backlinks |

**Our differentiation vs the twin (Great Indian Cocoa):**
1. **Deepest transparency** — public, per-batch, verifiable data (not just the word "traceable"). Publish a transparency report.
2. **Fermentation as flavour innovation** — Single Origin / Single Farm / **Creative Fermentation / Custom Fermentation** lots (a flavour R&D story competitors don't have).
3. **Premium brand + site craft** — the editorial/painterly experience signals quality before a spec is read.
4. **Proof via finished chocolate** — Manam / Almond House lineage shows the beans make award-worthy chocolate.
5. **Farmer-equity with numbers** — premium paid, payment within 24h, # of farmer-members.

## 4. Recommended site structure (IA)

Principle: **Home = overview that routes to depth. Each theme once. Every page ends in "Request a Sample / Quote."**

```
/                      Home — hook, proof, 4 doors, quote CTA
/products              Our Cacao (hub) — bean types + derivatives
  /products/single-origin        (per-lot spec pages live under here)
  /products/single-farm
  /products/creative-fermentation
  /products/custom-fermentation
  /products/cocoa-nibs
  /products/cocoa-powder
  /products/cocoa-butter
/our-craft             Origin (West Godavari) → Fermentery → post-harvest science → drying
/traceability          The transparency system + per-batch data + report PDF   ← differentiator
/community             100+ farmers, premiums, impact numbers (transparency highlights)
/wholesale             The B2B funnel: how to order, MOQ, Incoterms, lead times, packaging, countries, RFQ
/resources (Journal)   SEO guides that capture top-funnel search and funnel to quote
/about                 Story + Certifications & Compliance (COA, food safety, downloadable docs)
/connect               Contact + sample/quote form
```

**Navigation (lean, 5–6 items):** Products · Our Craft · Traceability · Community · Wholesale · _(Connect as button)_. Keep "Manam Webshop" only if it doesn't confuse B2B buyers (it currently reads retail).

**Primary CTA sitewide:** **"Request a Sample"** and **"Request a Quote (RFQ)"** — this is the single most important conversion element and is currently missing.

### The product/lot page = the SEO + conversion engine
Model after Cocoa Supply (rich origin pages) + Nutrada (spec filters). Each page should carry:
- Origin + region + farm, variety, **fermentation %, moisture, cut-test/defect, bean count, harvest date**
- Tasting notes (flavour wheel), recommended use (dark %, milk, etc.)
- Certifications, packaging options, **MOQ**, lead time
- "Request sample" + "Request quote" buttons
- Downloadable **spec sheet PDF** + COA-on-request note

### The /wholesale page = close the deal
Answer every buyer question from the sourcing checklist on one page: buying routes, MOQ tiers, price model, **Incoterms**, lead times, **packaging (jute/GrainPro 60–70kg)**, **countries served / export experience**, sample policy, compliance docs (phytosanitary, COA, FSVP/FDA). End with the RFQ form whose fields mirror a buyer's spec sheet:
`origin/variety preference · target flavour · volume · certifications needed · destination country · timeline`.

## 5. On-page & technical SEO checklist

**Foundations**
- Next.js SSR/SSG (already in place) → good crawlability. Add `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, canonical tags.
- Unique `<title>` + meta description per page, keyword-led. Examples:
  - Home: `Fine-Flavour Indian Cacao Beans — Farmer-Direct & Traceable | Origins Cocoa`
  - Products: `Buy West Godavari Cacao Beans Wholesale — Single Origin & Custom Fermentation`
  - A lot page: `Single-Origin West Godavari Cacao Beans (75% Fermented) — Wholesale`
- One clear `<h1>` per page containing the primary keyword.
- **Descriptive image alt text** — the painterly/mural art and (once added) real farm/fermentery photos.

**Structured data (schema.org JSON-LD)**
- `Organization` + `LocalBusiness` (Almond House address) on all pages
- `Product` + `Offer` on every product/lot page (with specs → eligible for rich results)
- `BreadcrumbList` sitewide
- `FAQPage` on /wholesale and guides
- `Article` on Journal posts

**Content / topical authority**
- Build the **Journal** to own "how to source fine-flavour cocoa", "fine flavour vs bulk", "reading a cut test", "Indian cocoa terroir — West Godavari". Internally link: guide → relevant product page → RFQ.
- Depth + real data (E-E-A-T): transparency numbers, certs, named farmers/fermentery, team, address, awards.

**International & off-page**
- Explicitly state **export markets served** (USA, Europe, Asia) — matches "wholesale cocoa beans [country]" intent. Add `hreflang` only if translating later.
- **List the brand** in B2B directories (wholesalecocoabeans.com, Nutrada, GlobalLinker, go4WorldBusiness) and craft-chocolate communities → referral traffic + backlinks.
- Pursue **Cocoa of Excellence / award** entries — buyers search "award-winning".

**Performance / UX**
- Keep the hero video lightweight (poster + reduced-motion fallback already done); lazy-load below-fold media; compress the large mural/stage PNGs (currently 5–8 MB → serve WebP/AVIF via next/image).
- Mobile-first: buyers browse on phones; keep spec tables and RFQ form thumb-friendly.

## 6. Presentation principles (how it should *feel*)

- **Clean, editorial, quiet confidence.** The premium look already signals quality — don't crowd it. White space + one idea per band.
- **Show, then specify.** Emotional origin story up top → hard specs one click away. Both audiences served.
- **Every page has an exit to conversion** (sample/quote), never a dead end.
- **Real proof beats adjectives.** Replace generic claims with numbers, certs, photos, and downloadable docs as they become available (the current placeholder slots are the right instinct — fill them with real farm/fermentery/lot photography, not stock).
- **Consistency = trust.** One type system, one colour system, one voice. (Fraunces display + Space Grotesk labels + Cormorant body is a solid, distinctive system.)
